Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 20, 2026
The short version: Verbull works fully as a guest, and used that way it collects nothing — all your words, progress, and stats stay on your device. The only time any data leaves your device is if you choose to sign in to use the optional social features (friends, duels, global leaderboard) — which also backs up your progress to your account. Even then, we don't run ads, we don't track you across apps or websites, and we never sell your data.

Summary

  • Guest by default. Every learning feature — Word of the Day, the Verbull game, quizzes, flashcards, the widget — works without an account. Used this way, no data is collected.
  • On-device. Your bookmarks, quiz/flashcard progress, activity stats, streaks, and settings are stored on your device. They are never transmitted to us unless you sign in, in which case they are also backed up to your account so they survive a new phone (see below).
  • Signing in is opt-in. The social features require Sign in with Apple. Only then do we collect the limited information described below, and only to make the social features work.
  • No ads, no trackers, no cross-app tracking, no third-party analytics SDKs.

Using Verbull without an account

If you never sign in, the app has no way to identify you and sends us nothing. The following is saved locally on your device and never leaves it:

  • Bookmarks — the terms you save.
  • Learning progress — quiz results, streaks, spaced-repetition scheduling, and the Verbull game's stats.
  • Activity stats — the on-device history that powers your Profile dashboard's trends.
  • Preferences — such as your daily-reminder setting and onboarding state.

Deleting the app removes all of it.

Signing in (optional)

Verbull includes optional social features — a friends feed, challenges and duels, a co-op weekly board, and a global leaderboard — plus a backup of your progress. To use them you sign in with Sign in with Apple. If (and only if) you sign in, we collect and store the following to operate those features:

  • Your name — as provided by Sign in with Apple the first time you sign in. If it isn't provided, a default display name ("Player") is used. Friends and game opponents see this name; you can change it any time in Settings → Account.
  • An account identifier — the anonymous user identifier from Sign in with Apple, plus a friend code (e.g. VERB-XXXX) we generate so friends can add you, and a private invite link/QR token you can share and regenerate.
  • Results you share with friends — a shared game result contains the mode, puzzle number, number of guesses, whether you won, an optional solve time, and a spoiler-safe block-grid (█▓░) of your guesses. The actual answer word is never included. Nothing is shared until you confirm your first share; after that, daily results can be shared automatically, and you can turn that off at any time in Settings.
  • Reactions — the emoji reactions you add to friends' posts.
  • Your connections — the friend links you create and any accounts you block.
  • Game boards you play with others — for friend challenges, the weekly co-op board, and duels against another player: your guess count and the colour pattern of your guesses (never the letters). In a duel, your display name is shown to the other player only after you have both finished.
  • Your progress backup — your streaks, game boards, flashcard/review progress, bookmarks, and per-day activity history, saved to your account so they survive a new phone. Only you can read your backup.
  • Global leaderboard (separate opt-in) — if you choose to join, a public handle you pick and your games played, games won, and longest streak, visible to all players. You can rename or leave at any time.
  • Push token (separate opt-in) — if you turn on "Friend activity" notifications, your device's push token, kept only while that setting is on. It is removed when you turn the setting off, sign out, or delete your account.

We do not request or receive your email address. Sign in with Apple can hide your email behind a private relay; Verbull doesn't ask for email at all.

Where this is stored. This information is held in our backend database, hosted by Supabase (a third-party infrastructure provider acting as our data processor on our behalf). It is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected by row-level security so that only you and your friends can see your posts. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or use it to track you across other apps or websites.

What we never collect

Regardless of whether you sign in, we do not collect your location, your contacts or address book (friends are added by code, QR, or invite link, never by reading your contacts), advertising identifiers, or any data for cross-app/website tracking.

Retaining and deleting your data

  • On-device data is removed when you delete the app.
  • Account data: you can sign out at any time. Choosing "Delete account" (Profile → Settings → Account) permanently removes your profile, posts, reactions, friend connections, challenge/duel/co-op boards, progress backup, leaderboard entry, and push tokens from our backend. This cannot be undone.

Purchases

The app offers "Pro" as a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We never see or receive your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. The app only receives a yes/no entitlement indicating whether you own Pro.

Notifications

If you opt in to the daily reminder, the app schedules local notifications on your device. These are generated on-device by iOS; no notification data is sent to any server.

If you sign in and turn on "Friend activity" notifications, your device's push token is stored in our backend and used with Apple's push service to notify you when a friend challenges you, accepts a request, or takes their turn. Turn the setting off at any time and the token is deleted.

Audio

Word pronunciations are produced on-device using Apple's built-in speech synthesizer. No audio is recorded, uploaded, or processed off your device.

Children's privacy

Verbull is rated 4+ and contains no ads and no cross-app tracking. The optional account uses Sign in with Apple. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond the limited account data described above, which is only ever created when a user signs in.

Data security

On-device data is protected by your device's standard iOS protections. Friends-feed data is transmitted over encrypted connections and access-controlled by row-level security so that data is only visible to you and the friends you've added.

Third parties

  • Apple — for App Store purchases (StoreKit), Sign in with Apple, and (if enabled) push notifications.
  • Supabase — our backend host / data processor for the optional signed-in features.

We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
