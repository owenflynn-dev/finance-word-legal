Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 28, 2026

The short version: we don't collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. The app has no servers and no accounts. Everything it stores stays on your device.

Summary

  • No account. You don't sign up or log in.
  • No data collection. The app sends none of your information to us or anyone else.
  • No analytics, ads, trackers, or third-party data SDKs.
  • Everything is on-device. Bookmarks, learning progress, settings, and reading history never leave your iPhone.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, advertising identifiers, usage analytics, or any other personal information. Because the app has no backend, there is nowhere for such data to be sent.

Information stored on your device

To make the app work, the following is saved locally using Apple's standard on-device storage and is never transmitted to us:

  • Bookmarks — the terms you save.
  • Learning progress — quiz results, streaks, and spaced-repetition scheduling.
  • Preferences — such as your daily-reminder setting and onboarding state.

This data remains under your control. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Purchases

The app offers a one-time "Pro" purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We never see or receive your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. The app only receives a yes/no entitlement indicating whether you own Pro.

Notifications

If you opt in to the daily reminder, the app schedules local notifications on your device. These are generated on-device by iOS; no notification data is sent to any server, and no push service is used.

Audio

Word pronunciations are produced on-device using Apple's built-in speech synthesizer. No audio is recorded, uploaded, or processed off your device.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It contains no ads and no data collection, and is suitable for general audiences.

Data security

Because no personal data leaves your device, there is no server-side data to be secured or breached. On-device data is protected by your device's standard iOS protections.

Third parties

The app integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only third party involved is Apple, solely for processing App Store purchases as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the revised policy at this URL.
